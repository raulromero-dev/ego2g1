my walk
of life

Everyone tells me that I walk in a particular way... just like my dad did.

My shoes do get a weird shape after a while

So I decided to check if it's true that I walk differently

I compared half an hour of recordings walking at SEAS to 100s of open source human demos.

Time was short, so my steps had 39% less variance than the open source dataset I used.

Turns out
it's true

My heels walk a tightrope.
My toes don't.

I was sure I planted my feet wide. At the ankle I'm the opposite.

So I turned my walk into robot training data.

Then I trained two robots

One learned from my 93 walks. The other learned from actors.

Neither one plants its feet like me. But one of them keeps my time.
